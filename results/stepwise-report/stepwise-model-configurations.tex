\textbf{01} & 2023 diagnostic-model refit & 2023 diagnostic-model refit & Provide a reproducible reference for subsequent comparisons. \\
\textbf{02} & New executable and INI 1007 & New MFCL executable (2.2.7.9-based) and INI 1007 & Isolate the combined executable and required INI-format update. \\
\textbf{03} & Natural mortality fixed & Lorenzen natural-mortality scaling fixed to the 2023 diagnostic estimate & Improve model stability as recommended at the PAW. \\
\textbf{04} & Length-weight update & BET 2026 bias-corrected length-weight parameters & Update biomass conversion for the 2026 assessment. \\
\textbf{05} & Five-region structure & Five-region, 33-fishery structure and reporting-rate remapping & Represent the revised spatial and fishery definitions and rebuild the current reporting-rate controls for the new fishery map. \\
\textbf{06} & Weight-as-length compositions & Reweighted weight data converted to length frequencies through 2021 & Evaluate conversion of reweighted weight data to length. \\
\textbf{07} & Observed-length supplementation & Weight-as-length plus observed-length compositions & Use observed lengths where their catch coverage exceeded weight samples. \\
\textbf{08} & Data through 2024 & Data updated through 2024 except CAAL & Extend the temporal coverage of the assessment. \\
\textbf{09} & PH/ID and domestic size-data rules & PH/ID and domestic mixed-gear size-data rules & Apply the agreed PH/ID and domestic mixed-gear size-data treatment. \\
\textbf{10} & Regional CPUE and scaling & Regional CPUE indices and regional scaling & Allow region-specific indices while penalising inconsistent regional abundance scaling. \\
\textbf{11} & Time-varying CPUE uncertainty & Time-varying CPUE uncertainty & Account for temporal variation in CPUE precision. \\
\textbf{12} & MLE-based regional CPUE SDs & Regional CPUE observation-error SDs fixed at their maximum-likelihood estimates (0.35, 0.24, 0.21, 0.24 and 0.23) & Retain the five maximum-likelihood CPUE observation-error SDs. \\
\textbf{13} & New CAAL data & New CAAL data with weight 0.75 & Use the 2023 BET age-data weighting as the reference treatment. \\
\textbf{14a} & Regional CAAL reweighting & Regional CAAL reweighting & Test region-level spatial CAAL weighting. \\
\textbf{14b} & Sub-basin CAAL reweighting & Sub-basin CAAL reweighting with regions 3 and 4 combined & Apply the selected sub-basin CAAL weighting. \\
\textbf{15} & Selectivity update & Flexible fishery-specific selectivity plus weak F10 non-decreasing penalty & Apply the selected flexible-selectivity treatment and deterministic weak F10 tail-stability penalty to the revised 33-fishery structure. \\
\textbf{16} & KS D-statistic tag mixing & Release-group-specific tag-mixing periods estimated using a KS D-statistic cutoff of 0.20 & Apply the release-group-specific mixing periods estimated for the 2026 assessment using a KS D-statistic cutoff of 0.20 (Scutt Phillips et al., 2026). \\
\textbf{17} & Pre-mixing reporting-rate exclusion & Pre-mixing reporting-rate exclusion & Avoid applying reporting rates in pre-mixing windows. \\
\textbf{18} & Effort-creep adjustment & Effort-creep adjustment & Account for gradual changes in fishing efficiency. \\
\textbf{19} & DM composition weighting & Dirichlet-multinomial composition weighting & Use DM-noRE G8/Nmax25 composition weighting with concentration intercepts fixed at 7. \\
\textbf{20} & Tag tau fixed at 2 & Negative-binomial tag tau fixed at 2 & Evaluate direct fixed tau=2 from the ordinary-makepar Step 19 basis. \\
\textbf{21} & F33 weak non-decreasing penalty & Weak F33 non-decreasing selectivity penalty & Stabilize F33 selectivity where Region 5 length-frequency information is limited: a weak non-decreasing penalty discourages unsupported dome-shaped tails without forcing strongly asymptotic selectivity, so the fitted shape remains data informed. \\
\textbf{22} & BET 2026 Diagnostic model & Steepness fixed at 0.90 & Adopt fixed h=0.90 and reproduce the current public BET 2026 Diagnostic model. \\
